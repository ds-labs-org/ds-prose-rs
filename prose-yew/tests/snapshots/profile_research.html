Agreement urn:policy:research-1

An agreement between http://example.com/org:hospital (assigner) and http://example.com/org:university (assignee).

  • Written to the profile Research Data Profile (https://example.org/odrl/profile/research, version 0.1.0).

  1. http://example.com/org:university may fine-tune http://example.com/data:cohort-2026, as permitted by http://example.com/org:hospital.

    Limits on the action:

    • The action "fine-tune" is limited to cases where:
      • the number of data subjects is at least 50

    Applies only if:

    • the research purpose is any of clinical research or academic research
    • the retention period is at most P2Y

  2. http://example.com/org:university may distill http://example.com/data:cohort-2026, as permitted by http://example.com/org:hospital.

  3. http://example.com/org:university must not commercialize http://example.com/data:cohort-2026, as prohibited by http://example.com/org:hospital.

  4. http://example.com/org:university must pseudonymise http://example.com/data:cohort-2026, as required by http://example.com/org:hospital.
